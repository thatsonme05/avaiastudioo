Membership Policy

Last updated:

1. Types of Membership

Avaia Studio offers several types of membership, including a single-class package, a first-timer package, and monthly packages. Full pricing and details for each package can be found on the Membership page.

2. Membership Terms

  1. Membership is activated on the date of purchase.
  2. Packages are valid according to the stated validity period.
  3. Membership is valid for all regular classes unless otherwise stated.
  4. Membership cannot be paused.
  5. Expired packages cannot be reinstated.
  6. All purchases are final and non-refundable.

3. Renewal & Payment

  • Membership renewal is done manually by the member through the dashboard or directly at the studio.
  • Payment is made in advance through the methods available in the system (Qris, e-wallet, bank transfer via official payment gateway).
  • Any price changes will be communicated at least 14 days before taking effect, and will not affect members who are still within an active package period.

4. Transfer

Membership is personal and cannot be transferred to another person.

For questions about your membership status or remaining sessions, please contact us via our Feedback page. See also our Booking Policy and Cancellation Policy.

5. Policy Changes

Avaia Studio reserves the right to update this membership policy at any time for operational adjustments. The latest version will always be available on this page.
