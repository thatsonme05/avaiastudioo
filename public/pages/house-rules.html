House Rules

Last updated:

Welcome to Avaia Studio. To create a comfortable and mindful space for everyone, we kindly ask you to observe the following guidelines.

  1. Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your class begins.
  2. For safety and to avoid disrupting the class, late arrivals beyond 10 minutes after class starts may not be permitted to join.
  3. Please keep your mobile phone on silent mode during class.
  4. Wear comfortable workout attire suitable for yoga and movement.
  5. Bring your own water bottle to stay hydrated.
  6. Please respect the instructor, fellow members, and the studio environment.
  7. Inform the instructor before class if you are pregnant, have injuries, or any medical conditions.
  8. Keep the studio clean and return all equipment after use.
  9. Enjoy the space with mindfulness and kindness.
Questions about these guidelines? Contact us via our Feedback page.
