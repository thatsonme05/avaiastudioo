Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

1. Cancellation by Members

Members can cancel a class booking on their own through the Dashboard page, at least 2 hours before the class starts.

  • Cancelling more than 2 hours before class: the spot and fee can be returned as class credit.
  • Last-minute cancellations (less than 8 hours before class): fees already paid are non-refundable.
  • Not showing up without cancelling ("no-show") is treated as a class already used.

2. Cancellation by the Studio

Under certain circumstances (instructor unavailability, force majeure, insufficient minimum participants, etc.), the studio reserves the right to cancel or reschedule a class.

Affected members will be notified as soon as possible, with the option to be moved to another session or receive a full refund as class credit.

3. Refunds & Class Credit

  • Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 7–14 business days, depending on the payment method.
  • Class credit is valid for the duration of your active membership and can be used to book other classes.
Need help with a cancellation or refund? Contact us via our Feedback page or the contact details in the footer.

4. Policy Changes

Avaia Studio reserves the right to update this policy at any time. Changes take effect from the date they are published on this page.
