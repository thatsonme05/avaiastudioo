Booking Policy

Last updated:

1. Booking

To ensure every member has a fair opportunity to join our classes, please note the following:

  1. Advance booking is required for all classes.
  2. Bookings are subject to availability.

2. No Show

  • Failure to attend without prior cancellation will be considered a No Show and one class credit will be deducted.

3. Studio Policy

  • Avaia Studio reserves the right to reschedule or cancel classes when necessary.
  • Members will be notified as early as possible if any schedule changes occur.
For more on cancelling your own booking or refunds, see our Cancellation Policy. Questions? Contact us via our Feedback page.
